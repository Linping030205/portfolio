01  EQUITY RESEARCH & AI

AI-Driven Equity Research Agent — NVIDIA

NVIDIA's valuation was hard to model with standard dividend-based methods. I independently designed and built the LLM-powered research agent that pulls financial data and valuation inputs to draft equity research notes. Result: a reproducible report generator, later folded into the team's full research pipeline.

PythonLLMEquity Research

02  RISK ANALYTICS

Transformer-Based Credit Default Prediction

Credit-card default prediction has to weigh missed defaults against costly false positives. I designed the Feature-as-Token Transformer architecture and led the methodology and ablation comparisons. Result: 0.7814 ROC-AUC, with higher recall than a tuned Random Forest baseline.

PythonMachine LearningCredit RiskModel Evaluation

03  TREASURY & PORTFOLIO

Corporate Treasury Reserve Portfolio — Unilever (£500m)

A £500m treasury reserve has to balance yield against liquidity, duration, credit and FX risk. I helped define the portfolio constraints, collected data and documented the methodology. Result: a reserve structured for a 4.05% expected yield at 0.65-year duration.

About

Outside finance, I play piano (Grade 9) and the hulusi (Grade 10), swim, hike, and spend a lot of time exploring London. I tend to enjoy things that reward patience and consistency, whether that's learning a piece of music or getting a model a little better each time.
