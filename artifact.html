About

Coursework is complete and my dissertation is submitted; the result is pending. My undergraduate degree is from Nanjing Agricultural University (GPA 3.85/4.5, arithmetic average 90.42/100, top 13% of my cohort).

Outside of spreadsheets, I play piano (Grade 9) and the hulusi (Grade 10), and swim and hike when I get the chance. All three taught me the same lesson: progress comes from showing up for the unglamorous repetition — which is most of what good financial analysis is too.
